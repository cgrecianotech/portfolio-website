## (Almost) Immaculate Score!
In my [previous blog post](https://christiangreciano.com/blog/posts/2026/11/0030_passed-claude-certified-architect-foundations-ccar-f/) I wrote how the Claude Certified Architect - Foundations (CCAR-F) was a step up in difficulty compared to the Claude Certified Developer - Foundations (CCDV-F). One would expect, by name alone, that the CCAR-P should be more difficult than the CCAR-F, but that's not the case. Maybe that will change with the [updates to the certs that Anthropic is planning to do starting 7th December](https://www.claudecertifiedarchitects.com/blog/claude-exam-update-december-2026/), but until now people have reported that, if anything, CCAR-P is ***easier*** than the CCAR-F, and I would have to agree with that.

In my case, I ended up using the whole 2 hours allotted for the exam, and my score was **981** points out of 1000 points. In the CCAR-F I scored 916, so indeed I felt CCAR-P was slightly easier. My CCAR-P score indicates that I most likely only failed a **single** question, which is annoying honestly! 😂 Mostly because I'm pretty sure which questions it was! I flagged two questions during the exam that I felt were very poorly worded, and I'm pretty sure it was one of the two, seeing which topic was the only one where I didn't score 100%... At least I left a comment for the Anthropic team to fix that question for future test takers, not sure how much they'll listen to me, though.

## Differences with CCAR-F
In AWS certs, there's a clear progression from Foundational to Associate to Professional certs in terms of difficulty and covered material. There is no such relationship between Anthropic's CCAR-F and the current CCAR-P. They both felt similar in difficulty, they both tested you on quite different topics, and the exam structure was also quite different.

In the CCAR-F exam, you are given 4 scenarios, with 15 questions per scenario. The scenarios are documented (you get 4 out of 6 documented scenarios), and go into nuances of the Anthropic SDK and building Anthropic agents.

In contrast, the CCAR-P exam does not have those common scenarios, and you're asked more generic questions about AI systems and everything surrounding them. Some examples: AI risks, RAG, stakeholder management, AI evaluations, and their nuances are much more important to know in the CCAR-P. All of these topics were covered quite a lot in the AWS Generative AI Developer - Professional (AWS AIP-C01) certification, which I [passed](https://christiangreciano.com/blog/posts/2026/1/0013_how-i-passed-aws-aip-genai-dev-pro-beta/) back in January, so maybe that's why I felt that CCAR-P was slightly easier than CCAR-F.

## Recommended Prep
So far my prep for the Anthropic certs has been quite light, relying mostly on Matthew Purcell's free practice exams that he has posted on LinkedIn. For CCAR-P I also recommend his [free practice exam (v2)](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/purcellmatthew_ccar-p-practice-exam-question-set-ugcPost-7522215192798142464-zF9t/). Without it, I probably would have still scored close to 900 points, but with it, I was close to acing the exam. His v2 practice exam feels very similar in difficulty and structure to the real thing. If, unlike me, you don't have that much job experience with these topics or haven't done other AI engineering certs, I definitely recommend taking a prep course (maybe the one in Anthropic's official training site).
