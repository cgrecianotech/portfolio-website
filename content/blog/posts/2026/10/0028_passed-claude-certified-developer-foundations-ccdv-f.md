I just passed the Claude Certified Developer - Foundations (CCDV-F)! 🥳 This marks my first Anthropic certification, and I'm grateful that I can take these via my employer Nitor, since Anthropic certifications are currently only available to Antrophic partners.

## It felt EZPZ
As someone who has done all AI certifications from AWS already, and who uses Claude Code daily for his work as a software engineer, this certification felt *really* easy to me. If you have a similar background to mine, you'll probably agree with me. I completed all 53 questions with 40 minutes to spare, and scored quite nicely: **941** (out of 1000 points).

<img src="{{ '0028_ccdv-f-score-sheet.png' | blogImagePath }}" alt="My detailed Score Sheet for the CCDV-F exam" eleventy:ignore>

For preparation, I only took [Matthew Purcell's free practice exam](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/purcellmatthew_claude-certified-developer-foundations-practice-ugcPost-7484423481447874560-XyDi). The questions felt really easy to me and I only failed two of them in his practice exam. Seeing this, I didn't bother to do any extra prep. Anthropic has a bunch of online courses you can take if you want to prepare more thoroughly though.

## Relevance of the Cert
Even though the exam felt super easy, I do believe **everything** that was being asked is very **relevant** to a software developer in the current AI era. I have encountered many of the proposed scenarios and problems in my day-to-day job, so this is not just an academic and theoretic certification. It's funny, because if I took this exam 3 years ago, I would have no clue how to answer these questions. But then again, we did things very differently in the software industry 3 years ago (Anthropic and Claude were very different back then too). Maybe that's also why Anthropic certs expire after one year. I hope Anthropic allows us to renew them without having to resit the exams!

## What's Next?
Nitor pays me to get certifications, and seeing what [Matthew Purcell](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/claude-certification-exams-honest-review-matthew-purcell-byo2e/) and others say about the Anthropic certs, I think it makes sense for me to get all four. I probably won't need to prepare a lot for any of the other 3 certifications either. Maybe I will even try the online proctored way! So far I have always gone to a test center for AWS certifications (and for this one too), but here in Helsinki your only options are almost always at 9 o'clock in the morning outside of the city, in Espoo. I often sleep badly when I have high stakes appointments in the morning! Seeing that these certs are not super demanding, it probably makes sense to take them from a dummy Lenovo Thinkpad laptop provided by my employer, at a more comfortable time of the day.

I also have in my mind to take the AWS AI Business Strategist (AIB-C01) certification soon, [even if I think it's nonsense for AWS to be the vendor of this cert](https://www.christiangreciano.com/blog/posts/2026/09/0027_aws-announces-new-ai-business-strategist-certification/). I noticed that both in Matthew Purcell's practice exam and in the CCDV-F exam itself I actually failed the questions having to do with business requirements. I probably need to refresh/relearn the business lingo!
